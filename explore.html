How to use

Tap or click a cell to highlight it on its own.

Long-press (touch) or right-click a cell to open a menu for highlighting a whole row, diagonal, or hockey stick instead.

Pinch (touch) or scroll the mouse wheel to zoom in and out.

Arrow keys pan the view, and + / - zoom in and out.
